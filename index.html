What Is "Optimization," In Plain Terms?

Right now, when you design something like a footing, you try one size, check if it's safe, and if it passes, you move on. Optimization means teaching your spreadsheet to try hundreds of sizes automatically, throw out anything that fails your own safety checks, and show you the cheapest and lowest-carbon option among the ones that pass.

Think of it like GPS for your design: it doesn't replace your engineering judgment—it just checks more routes than you could by hand, and shows you the best ones.

Today

  • Try 1–2 designs by hand
  • Pick whichever passes first
  • No way to know if it's the best option

With Optimization

  • Computer tries hundreds of designs
  • Only keeps ones that pass your own checks
  • You choose from the best-ranked options

Do I need to know how to code?

No. If you can already build a calculation spreadsheet, you already have the hard part. This training shows you how to let the computer repeat that logic thousands of times instead of once.
